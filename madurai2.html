Discover Madurai : The Heart of Tamil Culture

Madurai, known as the "Athens of the East," is a city rich in history and tradition. Famous for the iconic Meenakshi Amman Temple, it blends ancient culture with modern vibrancy.

From bustling markets to delicious food, Madurai offers a unique glimpse into Tamil heritage.

Must Visit Places

From Legends to Landmarks — Experience It All!

Meenakshi Amman Temple

A stunning temple in Madurai, dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi, known for its intricate architecture and divine aura.

Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal

A grand 17th-century palace showcasing the opulence of the Nayak dynasty in Madurai.

Theppakulam

A peaceful water tank with a temple in the center, reflecting ancient engineering marvels that hosts the TheppaThiruvizha.

Thousand Pillars

An architectural wonder with intricately carved 1000 pillars, each telling a divine story.

Keezhadi

An archaeological site revealing the ancient Tamil civilization’s heritage dating back to 6th century BCE.

Gandhi Museum

A museum honoring Mahatma Gandhi that narrates the life and legacy of India’s freedom struggle.

Chithirai Fest

A vibrant festival celebrating the divine wedding of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar, and Azhagar’s journey.

Alanganallur Jallikattu

Traditional bull-taming sport during Pongal, symbolizing bravery and Tamil culture.

Vaigai River

The lifeline of Madurai, adding beauty and spiritual value to the city’s landscape.
